# Shadow Run – Privacy Policy

*Effective 18 November 2026*

Shadow Run is a single-player maze adventure game for Android made by Sanjeeb Boyaju ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to **sanjeebboyaju@gmail.com**.

## What the game stores about you

**Your game progress.** The levels you have unlocked, your best times, the collectibles you have found and your settings (sound, music, vibration and similar options). This is saved in a file on your device only. It is not uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot see it.

**No account.** Shadow Run has no sign-in and no account. We do not ask for your name, your email address or any other contact details.

We do not collect your contacts, your photos, your files, your microphone or camera, or your precise location. The game has no chat, no online play and no in-app purchases.

## Advertising

Shadow Run is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob: banner ads, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional video ads that you can choose to watch for an in-game reward.

To show ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), information about your device, and information about how you interact with the ads, as well as crash and performance information about the ads SDK. Google uses this to show ads, to measure them, and to prevent fraud and abuse. This information goes to Google, not to us.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before AdMob uses your information for personalised ads, and you can change your choice later. Wherever you are, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Service providers

- **Google:** AdMob advertising, and Google Play for distributing the app. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Unity Technologies:** the game is built with the Unity engine. The engine itself may collect basic, non-identifying crash and device information. See the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

We do not sell your information. We do not receive or store any personal information ourselves.

## How long information is kept, and deleting it

Your game progress stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Both of these delete it for good. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own policy; you can reset your advertising ID at any time as described above.

## Children

Shadow Run is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and want related advertising data removed, contact us and we will help.

## Security

Information sent between the game and Google's ad services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
